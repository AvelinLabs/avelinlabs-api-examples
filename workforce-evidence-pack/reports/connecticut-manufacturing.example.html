Employer-validation workshop

  1. Which roles are currently hard to hire or retain, and at which locations?
  2. Which tasks must a worker perform independently within 30, 60 and 90 days?
  3. Which listed skills are true entry requirements versus trainable on the job?
  4. Which credentials, equipment and safety requirements are mandatory?
  5. How many hires, incumbent workers and completions would make training worthwhile?

Output gate: Record occupation retention, local demand evidence, required competencies, training outcome and accountable reviewer.
